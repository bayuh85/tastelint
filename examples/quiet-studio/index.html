Beaver Street · shared kiln

Stoneware fired on Mondays.

Kiln & Kin is a twelve-person studio. We throw, trim, and glaze in the same room, then open the order book for one week each month.

What goes in the kiln

Bowls, everyday cups, and a small run of lamps. The clay is a speckled stoneware from Sheffield. Glazes are mixed in-house: oat, iron red, and a pale celadon.
